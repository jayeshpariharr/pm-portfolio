Fleet hardware, software and parts marketplace

Client name withheld under NDA

Fleet operators buy GPS trackers, AIS-140 devices, dashcams, sensors and spare parts, often in bulk. The old store made that hard.

  • BeforeNo price on product cards, and a "Submit request" form instead of a buy button.
  • BeforePricing hidden in a separate tab, with one "Standard" plan and no bulk rates.
  • AfterPrice + GST and the best bulk rate on every card, bulk slabs on every product page, and a cart and checkout.
  1. Wrote the design rules first9 reference screens became a style guide with 27 rule IDs. Every colour and spacing token cites the rule it came from.
  2. Moved content into validated filesThe catalog lives in JSON with 20 content rules. The build stops if a bulk tier costs more than the tier before it.
  3. Added a design-compliance reviewerA read-only subagent checks new code against the rules. It caught all 3 violations I planted in a product card.
  4. Documented the project for the next sessionA fresh session answered 5 of 5 questions about the product from the project brief alone, without opening a file.
  5. Made vendor onboarding one commandA skill cleans a messy vendor CSV and adds it to the catalog. It rejects rows with no base price and flags odd bulk rates for the vendor.

Support operations

Support AI Assistant

AI agentsClaude skills
Problem
Five support jobs, from ticket triage to weekly QA. Which should be a reusable skill, which an agent, and which just a scheduled script?
Decision
2 skills (triage, policy-safe replies), 2 agents (at-risk customer briefing, bug check) and 1 plain batch job for QA. I built and tested the 4 AI pieces.
Measure
2/5 test tickets escalated at P1 (live outage, GDPR) · reply checker: 0 invented figures vs 3 in the draft · 2/2 bug reports routed right

Trust & Safety · short video

PulseGuard

PRDML signalsPolicy
Problem
One reel grew into a national protest in 72 hours with zero early warnings. The feed ranks by speed of engagement and can't tell fun-viral from a call to act.
Decision
Score only the fastest-growing 0.1% of reels on content, spread shape and source. Respond with graded, reversible distribution limits decided by people, not take-downs.
Measure
≥80% flagged before reaching 3 states · median upload-to-flag ≤3 hrs · guardrail: ≤15% of actions overturned

Food delivery · architecture

QuickBite: scaling from 5K to 2M users

System designTrade-offs
Problem
Leadership wants 2M users in 12 months. Engineering has to choose an architecture without paying for that scale on day one.
Decision
Launch as a modular monolith with read replicas. Extract live rider tracking first, only when its location pings start slowing down orders.
Measure
strong consistency for payments and order status · eventual for ratings and search · 4 incident drills written out

Food delivery · Tier-1 metros

Zomato: funnel, cohorts and North Star

FunnelCohortsMetric tree
Problem
Users drop off across a 7-stage journey, and many churn after their first discounted order.
Decision
Fix cart-to-checkout first by showing all fees upfront, since these users already spent 5–10 minutes choosing. For new users, a "Next 3 Meals" milestone track.
Measure
North Star: weekly on-time, well-rated orders per active user, replacing GMV · one hypothesis and guardrail per stage

Travel marketplace · India

Airbnb: growth for Tier 2/3 India

PLGGrowth loopsMonetisation
Problem
Domestic travel to smaller cities is growing, but listings there are thin. Would-be hosts drop off at English-only forms and bank-wire payouts.
Decision
P0: vernacular onboarding with UPI payouts, plus a WhatsApp host referral loop. Test with a static Hindi page and manual ops before building anything.
Measure
North Star: active earning hosts in Tier 2/3 · scale only if publish rate lifts >30% vs English control

Fitness app · retention

VitaFit Momentum

PRDUser flowsA/B test
Problem
Users start strong, then drop off after days 14–21. Missing one workout makes them feel guilty, and they quit.
Decision
Personal weekly goals with a forgiving "streak saver", plus instructor-led groups of up to 100 who pool minutes instead of competing on a leaderboard.
Measure
North Star: 4-week retention, +15% target · 5% beta, then a 6-week A/B test · guardrail: workout drop-out rate

Ride hailing · urban pickup

Uber: the pickup point problem

User interviewsProblem framing
Problem
After booking, riders can't find the right legal pickup spot in crowded areas. Drivers end up calling them, which causes delays and cancellations.
Decision
Guide riders before confusion starts: suggest the nearest legal pickup point, give walking directions to it, and add a shared orientation view for rider and driver.
Measure
10+ interviews with riders and drivers · roll out first in malls, stadiums and downtown areas

Beauty e-commerce

Nykaa: buying skincare by skin type

Journey mappingDiscovery
Problem
Search for "face mask for dry skin" works well, but filters are deeply nested, there's no side-by-side comparison, and sponsored listings crowd the top.
Decision
Auto-apply the user's skin profile as a filter, add a one-tap comparison of size, price per ml and ingredients, and show reviews from people with the same skin type first.
Measure
5-step journey: search → browse → filter → evaluate → buy · friction mapped at each step

Online travel · flight booking

MakeMyTrip: usability review

Nielsen heuristicsUX laws
Problem
The flight search shows every input at once with equal weight, error messages don't say how to fix them, and airport names rely on memory.
Decision
Order the search as Where → When → Who → Search, autocomplete city + airport + code, and put secondary flight details behind "View details".
Measure
7 usability issues, each mapped to a heuristic or a law (Hick's law, recognition over recall)
